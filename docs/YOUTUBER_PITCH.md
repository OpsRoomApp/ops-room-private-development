## The short version

OPS ROOM is a free Windows companion for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024. It connects flight planning, aircraft performance, dispatch, GSX ground services, live telemetry, replay, landing analysis, airline finances and community features in one operations centre.

The latest release also adds an in-game tablet panel, so the full OPS ROOM interface can be opened from the simulator toolbar without leaving the cockpit.

## A natural introduction

Hi,

I wanted to introduce you to OPS ROOM, a free flight operations companion for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024.

It is designed for people who enjoy the complete airline experience, not just the time spent airborne. OPS ROOM starts with the SimBrief flight plan, helps prepare the aircraft, follows the operation through boarding and pushback, records the flight, and produces a proper post-flight review. It runs beside the simulator, in a browser, on a tablet or phone over LAN, and now inside the simulator through an in-game tablet panel.

The idea is simple: instead of opening a collection of separate tools for every flight, you have one connected place for the information and workflows that matter.

## What the app covers

### Briefing and preparation

The Briefing module imports the SimBrief OFP and presents the route, weather, METAR and TAF information, precipitation radar, FAA NOTAMs, charts and airport information together. Procedures provides aircraft checklists and flows, while Scratchpad keeps notes with the flight.

The Performance module provides first-party takeoff and landing calculations across the supported fleet. It can produce V1, VR and V2, or VLS and VREF where appropriate, along with flap, trim, flex temperature, assumed temperature and runway distance guidance. Runway, wind, temperature, QNH and aircraft data are filled from SimBrief, live weather and the simulator wherever available.

### Dispatch and ground operations

Live OFP tracks planned and actual times, fuel, weights, passenger figures, cargo and other flight milestones. The loadsheet can be signed with a typed or drawn electronic signature before departure, and the completed flight can be reviewed and signed again after arrival.

Ground Control connects with GSX to coordinate boarding, catering, water, pushback and arrival services. Services are monitored and recorded as receipts, which gives the flight a useful operational history and feeds into the finance features.

FIDS and the dispatch views complete the airline operations side of the app and make it easy to see what is planned, what is active and what has already been completed.

### Flight monitoring and cockpit tools

Flight Watch provides a live view of the aircraft, position, phase and simulator connections. A shared telemetry path means Flight Watch, Black Box, Runway Awareness, announcements and flight analysis are working from the same picture rather than polling the simulator independently.

Runway Awareness provides RAAS-style callouts and closure alerts. CPDLC through Hoppie supports controller-pilot datalink, including logon, messages and PDC requests. The Announcer handles cabin and operational announcements, with volume that follows the camera view.

### In-game tablet panel

The in-game tablet is one of the main additions in v0.25. A toolbar button opens the full OPS ROOM interface inside MSFS 2020 or 2024 in a tablet-style frame. The panel includes the launcher and the complete module set, including status, FIDS, dispatch, briefing, scratchpad, flight watch, performance, runway awareness, network, live map, datalink, ground, announcer, procedures, logbook, Black Box, finances and OBS Tools.

It connects to the desktop app on the same PC, so the panel uses the same live data as the external browser interface. If OPS ROOM is not running, it displays a clear start screen and reconnects automatically when the app is launched. The Community package is included with the Windows distribution and supports the Store and Steam Community folder locations for both simulator versions.

This would make a particularly good part of a setup or full-flight video because it shows the app being used as part of the cockpit workflow rather than as another window beside the simulator.

### Black Box and replay

Black Box records the flight path, aircraft movement, controls, engines, systems, autopilot, flaps and gear at different recording rates throughout the flight. It reconstructs landing performance, including touchdown speed, vertical speed, G-loading and bounce detection.

The flight can then be replayed inside the simulator with pause, resume, scrubbing, looping and playback speed controls. Recordings can also be exported to CSV, GPX and KML. This gives landing review videos a complete record of the approach rather than a single landing-rate number.

### Debrief and airline management

The Logbook and PIREP provide a full post-flight review with runway profiles, stability gates, touchdown metrics, score details and passenger satisfaction. Finances adds an airline and pilot economy with revenue, costs, GSX receipts, balances and passenger response to the quality of the operation.

The result is a complete loop: plan the flight, operate it, review the result and see how it affects the airline.

### Maps, NOTAMs and community features

The map combines the aircraft and route with precipitation radar, FAA NOTAMs, TFR and FDC information, airspace and local airport surface data. NOTAM closure markers can be installed as a Community package for MSFS 2020 and 2024, including runway and taxiway X markers, barricades and a lighted closure trailer.

The optional Discord integration provides Rich Presence, takeoff and landing posts, a public leaderboard and a live community map. Pilots choose what they share. OBS Tools provide overlays for creators who want to show flight data during a stream.

OPS ROOM integrates with SimBrief, GSX, the Fenix A320, PMDG 777, FSUIPC, SimConnect, VATSIM and vPilot, ChartFox and Hoppie CPDLC. Aircraft adapters are available for supported add-on aircraft where extra aircraft data is needed.

## Suggested full-flight video structure

1. Open with a completed landing and a short Black Box replay.
2. Introduce OPS ROOM and show the in-game tablet panel inside the simulator.
3. Load a SimBrief OFP and review the weather, route, NOTAMs and charts.
4. Prepare the aircraft with the Performance calculator and loadsheet sign-off.
5. Start the operation with FIDS, dispatch and GSX ground services.
6. Fly with Flight Watch, the live map, Runway Awareness, CPDLC and announcements.
7. Review the approach and landing using Black Box replay and landing metrics.
8. Complete the PIREP, logbook review, electronic sign-off and finance update.
9. Finish with Discord, leaderboard or OBS features if they fit the channel.
