OPS ROOM SCORING RULES

OPS ROOM scores the recorded flight only; raw telemetry is retained for scoring.

  • Departure: takeoff roll, runway centreline, liftoff attitude and initial climb.
  • Enroute: recorded deviations, simulation integrity and fuel/distance consistency.
  • Approach: 1000 ft/500 ft stability gates, lateral deviation and glidepath deviation.
  • Landing: touchdown rate, touchdown zone, centreline deviation and rollout.
  • Bounces: minor ?2, moderate ?5, severe/multiple ?8, capped at ?12. Small 1-2 ft radio-altimeter noise is ignored.
