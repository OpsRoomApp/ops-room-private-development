OPS ROOM SCORING RULES

This page documents the actual PIREP PRO V1 calculation used by the current analysis engine. The final score is out of 100 and is the sum of five category scores after deductions.

PIREP PRO V1 · 0.25.52

Score Formula

Overall = Departure + Enroute + Approach + Landing + Integrity
Maximum = 15 + 20 + 25 + 25 + 15 = 100
Grade bands: Excellent ?92 · Very Good ?84 · Good ?74 · Acceptable ?62 · Review Required <62

If advanced analysis cannot validate a metric, OPS ROOM should mark that metric unavailable instead of publishing fake zeroes or impossible values. Older fallback debrief scoring may still be used only when the advanced analysis result is unavailable.
