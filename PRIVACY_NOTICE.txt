OPS ROOM - PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS OF SERVICE (v0.25.0)

1. LOCAL STORAGE & DATA PRIVACY
All flight logs, kneeboard notes, custom charts, and aircraft profile settings are saved locally on your personal machine. OPS ROOM does not harvest or sell personal identifying information.

2. NETWORK SERVICES
OPS ROOM connects to external APIs (SimBrief, VATSIM, ChartFox, and opsroom.live) strictly to fetch flight plans, METAR weather, and active schedules.

3. SIMULATOR INTEGRATION
Local flight data is read directly from your simulator via SimConnect / FSUIPC solely to render live flight instrumentation.

4. DISCLAIMER
OPS ROOM IS INTENDED FOR FLIGHT SIMULATION USE ONLY. DO NOT USE FOR REAL-WORLD AVIATION NAVIGATION.
